Privacy Policy — Mantra Mandiram

Last updated: 2026

Mantra Mandiram ("the App") is a devotional reference app for mantras, stotras and related content in Kannada, Sanskrit, Tamil, Malayalam and Telugu. This page explains what data the App does and does not handle.

What we collect

Nothing. The App does not require an account, does not collect your name, email, location, contacts, or any other personal information, and does not run analytics or advertising trackers of any kind.

What's stored on your device

Your selected language and any deities you mark as favorites are saved using your browser's local storage, directly on your own device. This information is never transmitted anywhere — it stays on your phone and is used only to remember your preferences between visits. Uninstalling the App removes it.

Third-party services

The App loads typefaces from Google Fonts to render Kannada, Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam and Devanagari text correctly. Loading a font, like loading any web resource, causes your device's IP address to be visible to that server as part of normal internet requests. No other third-party service is used.

Children

The App contains educational religious content suitable for family and child use, and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address.
